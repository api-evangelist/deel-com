Hi Developer community, we’ve got another exciting update to share today! We’re announcing the new Webhook Simulation —a new capability designed to make testing and validating your webhook integrations safer, easier, and more reliable. With this feature, you can now simulate webhook events directly from the webhook management interface, without triggering any real business processes or impacting live systems.
