Hello @Steve.Price You can rely on Get Policies for profile for checking the allowances for a specific user, and Get Profile entitlements for the checking the usage of those allowances. But to modify it, you must do it via the UI. You can go to Organizations Settings → Time-off And from there you can create a new policy and/or assign a worker to a specific policy.
