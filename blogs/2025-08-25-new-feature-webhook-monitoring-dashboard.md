Hi Developer community, we’ve got some exciting news to share today! We recently released the Webhook Monitoring Dashboard —a new interface that provides detailed visibility into webhook events, statuses, and payloads, making it easier for you to debug, monitor, and optimize webhook performance. Here’s what you can do with it: Master-Detail Interface: View a list of webhook attempts and click for full inspection, including payloads, headers, response codes, and retry history.
